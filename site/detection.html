Model comparison

Selected by cross-validation, then scored on the OPSSAT-AD test split

The model is chosen by 5-fold cross-validation on the training split only (CV AUC-PR column), never by test results. The test columns are scored once, at the end. AUC-PR is the headline metric because anomalies are only about 21% of segments, and MCC stays fair under that imbalance. Rows marked paper are published baselines from the OPS-SAT benchmark paper, on the same test split.

Inside the AI: 120 trees vote

Every frame is computed live in your browser by the deployed model. A real OPS-SAT window, sometimes with an injected fault, is measured, and each of the 120 trees casts its vote (red for anomaly, green for nominal). The left column shows which families of measurements drove the result.
