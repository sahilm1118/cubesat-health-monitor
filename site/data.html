Data & reproducibility

Real datasets from flight and the lab

Every figure on this page is computed by the project's training script from the datasets below. The script uses a fixed random seed (42), so rerunning it produces the same numbers.

NASA Li-ion Battery Aging

Source
NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence battery testbed
Contents
Conditions
4 °C, 22–24 °C and 43–44 °C ambient; 1, 2 and 4 A discharge; 1.5 A CC/CV charge to 4.2 V
Cleaning
Zero and outlier runs removed (NASA notes unexplained low-capacity runs); smoothed with a 5-run rolling median
End of life
Capacity below 80% of the cell's initial capacity (industry-standard SOH-80)
License
U.S. government public data
Link
data.nasa.gov · Li-ion Battery Aging Datasets

Saha, B., Goebel, K. Battery Data Set. NASA Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA (2007).

Reproduce every number

Run
python scripts/train_models.py
Then
python scripts/build_site.py
Check
node scripts/check_js.cjs
Seeds
Fixed (42 for models; 11 and 12 for the final injection tests)

The browser runs the same model as the Python pipeline. The parity test gives identical alarms and anomaly types on 150 of 150 real segments.

Telemetry: ESA OPS-SAT via the OPSSAT-AD dataset (CC BY 4.0). Battery data: NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence.

Models built with scikit-learn. Charts drawn with Chart.js.
