Remaining useful life, with 90% range

The shaded band is a conformal 90% prediction interval. Its width is learned only from the other cells' leave-one-out errors.

State of health from a partial discharge

Uses only the first 15 minutes of each discharge (voltage at set times, voltage slope, temperature rise), plus load current and temperature. A CubeSat in eclipse rarely drains its battery fully, so the estimate can't rely on a full discharge.
