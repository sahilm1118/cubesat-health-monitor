Score your own telemetry

Paste or upload a CSV with one value per line, or time,value pairs with time in seconds (8 to 5,000 samples). Choose which OPS-SAT sensor's normal profile to compare against. The file is read in your browser and only the numbers are sent to the analysis server. If the server is offline, scoring runs in your browser instead.
